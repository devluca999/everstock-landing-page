05

Guide line

One hairline, snapped to a lattice column, that draws itself down the page as you scroll. It ties the sections together without adding another effect.

01 · Hero 02 · Who it is for 03 · How it works 04 · You stay in control
Scroll inside the frame
Live · {{ guideReadout }}
Stroke1px on a whole pixel. It never scales, tapers or glows.
ColourGuide token: rgba(242,241,237,0.34) on graphite, rgba(22,23,27,0.34) on eggshell. Never blue, because the line is not Everstock doing anything.
PositionSits exactly on a lattice column: the second column from the content edge on desktop, the first on mobile. Section labels hang 16px to its right.
DrawLength equals scroll position plus 60% of the viewport, so the tip leads the reader. Tied directly to scroll: no easing, no overshoot. Retracts when you scroll back up.
LayerAbove the lattice and below all content. Boxes and labels cover it where they overlap.
ReducedDrawn at full length from the start, static.
